Privacy Policy – Kingdoms of Old

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Kingdoms of Old is an app by Saru Bureau (Maassluis, the Netherlands, Chamber of Commerce no. 53787110).

What data do we collect?

None. The app does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. No account is needed, there are no ads, no analytics or tracking software is used and the app does not connect to the internet.

What is stored on your device?

Only your campaign progress and your camera setting. They stay on your own device and are removed when you delete the app.

Children

Because the app collects no data, no data is collected from children either.
