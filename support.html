Kingdoms of Old – Support

Have a question, a problem or an idea for the game? Email info@sarubureau.nl. When reporting a problem, please include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Controls

  • One finger: pan the map, tap to select
  • Pinch with two fingers: zoom
  • Twist with two fingers: rotate the camera
  • Compass button: turn the camera back to north
  • Camera angle: adjustable in the pause menu
